REVISION SUMMARY

3. Improved Simulated Annealing / Genetic Algorithm behavior for full or over-demand datasets by allowing feasible swaps and assigned/unassigned exchanges.
4. Added dataset-size-aware iteration budgets so the web request does not appear frozen on larger manifests.
5. Replaced the built-in benchmark profiles with deterministic operationally realistic synthetic scenarios:
   - Demo: 12 / 12

   - Medium: 176 / 160

6. Added scenario/route metadata for the built-in datasets.
7. Added recommended-plan exports in CSV, JSON, Excel, Word, PDF and ZIP bundle.
8. Added responsive local-development CORS support for any localhost Vite port.
9. Kept the existing seven-algorithm architecture and existing constraint framework.

IMPORTANT ACADEMIC WORDING
The built-in datasets are realistic synthetic benchmark scenarios. Do not claim that they are actual proprietary shipping-line manifests. The route and operational characteristics are modeled to make optimization non-trivial and suitable for academic evaluation.
